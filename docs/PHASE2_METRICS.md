## Headline numbers

- Evaluated on **414,420 transactions** across 3 held-out synthetic MFS networks (39,494 accounts, 981 labelled suspicious). All networks together (train, validation, test): 832,356 transactions. Phase 1 used 156.
- Best model (gradient boosting + graph features): **precision 94.4%, recall 83.0%, F1 0.883, PR-AUC 0.937, false-positive rate 0.12%**.
- Rules-only baseline: precision 26.3%, recall 30.1%, F1 0.280, PR-AUC 0.202, false-positive rate 2.15%.
- Phase 1 Cygnus score on the same data: F1 0.063, PR-AUC 0.085. Current product score: F1 0.743, PR-AUC 0.796.
- 83 features per account in 5 families: transactional 18, temporal 14, graph 18, neighbourhood 30, account_type 3.

## Bias check across account tiers

False-positive rate = share of legitimate accounts of that tier that get an alert.

| Tier | Legitimate accounts | Rules FPR | Phase 1 score FPR | Current Cygnus score FPR | GBM + graph, no account type | GBM + graph |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| personal | 35,031 | 1.76% | 0.06% | 0.85% | 0.12% | 0.12% |
| agent | 1,412 | 5.03% | 96.95% | 2.62% | 0.42% | 0.50% |
| merchant | 2,070 | 6.76% | 27.34% | 1.69% | 0.00% | 0.00% |

Largest gap in false-positive rate between tiers: rules_baseline 5.00 points, phase1_composite 96.89 points, cygnus_composite 1.77 points, gbm_graph_no_account_type 0.42 points, gbm_graph 0.50 points.

- By district (14 districts, legitimate personal accounts), rules_baseline: false-positive rate between 1.40% and 2.12%; chi-square p = 0.907 (no evidence of dependence on district).
- By district (14 districts, legitimate personal accounts), gbm_graph: false-positive rate between 0.00% and 0.28%; chi-square p = 0.173 (no evidence of dependence on district).

The Phase 1 score flagged almost every legitimate agent, because collecting from many customers and turning money around quickly is what an agent does. The current score is tier-aware: fan-in, fan-out and rapid movement are not counted against agents and merchants, and their statistical outlier score is replaced by the trained classifier. District is never a model input. Account type (personal / agent / merchant) is a model input in gbm_graph and left out of gbm_graph_no_account_type, so the effect of giving the model the account type can be read from the two columns.

## Analyst workload estimate

Assumption: 20 minutes of analyst review per alert (an assumption, not a pilot measurement). Figures are for the 39,494 held-out accounts.

- Rules: 1,123 alerts, 295 true positives (recall 30.1%), 374 analyst hours.
- Graph model: 862 alerts, 814 true positives (recall 83.0%), 287 analyst hours.
- To find as many suspicious accounts as the rules do, the graph model needs 295 alerts: **73.7% fewer alerts at equal recall**.
- With the same number of alerts as the rules, the graph model reaches 90.2% recall.
- Review time per true positive: rules 76 minutes, graph model 21 minutes.

## Limitations

- All data is synthetic. The simulator and the detectors were built by the same team, so these numbers are an upper bound on what to expect from real upay traffic.
- Suspicious accounts are about 2.5% of the test networks, far more common than in production; precision on real data would be lower at the same threshold.
- Labels are exact by construction. Real labels (filed STRs, confirmed fraud) are delayed, incomplete and noisy.
- The neighbourhood features are fixed 2-hop averages, not a trained graph neural network; a learned GNN is the next step once real labelled data is available.
- The analyst-workload figures assume 20 minutes of review per alert. That is an assumption, not a measurement from a pilot.
