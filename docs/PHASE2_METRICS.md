## Headline numbers

- Evaluated on **423,042 transactions** across 3 held-out synthetic MFS networks (39,490 accounts, 1,010 labelled suspicious). All networks together (train, validation, test): 848,752 transactions. Phase 1 used 156.
- Best model (gradient boosting + graph features): **precision 94.8%, recall 83.8%, F1 0.890, PR-AUC 0.943, false-positive rate 0.12%**.
- Rules-only baseline: precision 26.5%, recall 32.1%, F1 0.290, PR-AUC 0.201, false-positive rate 2.35%.
- Phase 1 Cygnus score on the same data: F1 0.129, PR-AUC 0.101. Current product score: F1 0.778, PR-AUC 0.838.
- 83 features per account in 5 families: transactional 18, temporal 14, graph 18, neighbourhood 30, account_type 3.

- Customers who only visited a risk area: 8 of 1,027 flagged.

Both detectors compare an account with its own history. A takeover alert needs at least two signals (area new for this customer, new handset, unusually fast outflow, new receivers); a hundi alert needs informal funding, a payout to five or more beneficiaries and the same beneficiaries paid in two or more cycles. The simulated hundi and takeover cases are clean, so these figures are an upper bound.

## Bias check across account tiers

False-positive rate = share of legitimate accounts of that tier that get an alert.

| Tier | Legitimate accounts | Rules FPR | Phase 1 score FPR | Current Cygnus score FPR | GBM + graph, no account type | GBM + graph |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| personal | 35,013 | 1.96% | 0.43% | 1.08% | 0.12% | 0.11% |
| agent | 1,397 | 5.44% | 98.71% | 0.57% | 0.64% | 0.57% |
| merchant | 2,070 | 6.91% | 26.43% | 0.00% | 0.00% | 0.00% |

Largest gap in false-positive rate between tiers: rules_baseline 4.95 points, phase1_composite 98.28 points, cygnus_composite 1.08 points, gbm_graph_no_account_type 0.64 points, gbm_graph 0.57 points.

- By district (14 districts, legitimate personal accounts), rules_baseline: false-positive rate between 1.46% and 2.60%; chi-square p = 0.197 (no evidence of dependence on district).
- By district (14 districts, legitimate personal accounts), gbm_graph: false-positive rate between 0.00% and 0.24%; chi-square p = 0.298 (no evidence of dependence on district).

The Phase 1 score flagged almost every legitimate agent, because collecting from many customers and turning money around quickly is what an agent does. The current score is tier-aware: fan-in, fan-out and rapid movement are not counted against agents and merchants, and their statistical outlier score is replaced by the trained classifier. District is never a model input. Account type (personal / agent / merchant) is a model input in gbm_graph and left out of gbm_graph_no_account_type, so the effect of giving the model the account type can be read from the two columns.

## Analyst workload estimate

Assumption: 20 minutes of analyst review per alert (an assumption, not a pilot measurement). Figures are for the 39,490 held-out accounts.

- Rules: 1,228 alerts, 324 true positives (recall 32.1%), 409 analyst hours.
- Graph model: 892 alerts, 846 true positives (recall 83.8%), 297 analyst hours.
- To find as many suspicious accounts as the rules do, the graph model needs 324 alerts: **73.6% fewer alerts at equal recall**.
- With the same number of alerts as the rules, the graph model reaches 92.9% recall.
- Review time per true positive: rules 76 minutes, graph model 21 minutes.

## Limitations

- All data is synthetic. The simulator and the detectors were built by the same team, so these numbers are an upper bound on what to expect from real upay traffic.
- Suspicious accounts are about 2.5% of the test networks, far more common than in production; precision on real data would be lower at the same threshold.
- Labels are exact by construction. Real labels (filed STRs, confirmed fraud) are delayed, incomplete and noisy.
- The neighbourhood features are fixed 2-hop averages, not a trained graph neural network; a learned GNN is the next step once real labelled data is available.
- The analyst-workload figures assume 20 minutes of review per alert. That is an assumption, not a measurement from a pilot.
